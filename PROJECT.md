# Vywee

> **The world's most trusted buying decision platform.**

---

# Mission

Help people make confident buying decisions by bringing trusted research, AI insights, comparisons, live offers and community opinions into one platform.

---

# Vision

People should never need to visit multiple websites before purchasing a product.

Vywee combines:

- AI Research
- Product Comparisons
- Buying Guides
- Live Offers
- Price History
- Community Opinions
- In-Store Assistant

into one seamless experience.

---

# Core Philosophy

Vywee is NOT:

- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Coupon Website
- Cashback Website

Vywee IS:

- Buying Research Platform
- Decision Intelligence Platform

---

# Product Principles

Every feature must answer one question:

> Does this help users make better buying decisions?

If the answer is **No**, the feature should not be built.

---

# Core Values

1. Trust before Revenue
2. Research before Shopping
3. Explain every recommendation
4. Show reasons NOT to buy
5. AI assists, humans decide
