# Product Principles

> This document defines the non-negotiable principles that every feature, design decision, engineering decision, AI workflow, and business decision must follow throughout the lifetime of Vywee.

---

# Purpose

This document acts as the constitution of Vywee.

If any future feature, design, AI recommendation or business decision conflicts with these principles, these principles take priority.

---

# Mission

Help people make confident buying decisions through trusted research, transparent AI, and unbiased recommendations.

---

# Vision

Become the world's most trusted buying decision platform.

Users should never need to visit ten different websites before deciding what to buy.

---

# Core Philosophy

Vywee is NOT an e-commerce platform.

Vywee is NOT a cashback platform.

Vywee is NOT a coupon website.

Vywee is NOT a marketplace.

Vywee exists to help users make better buying decisions.

---

# Guiding Question

Every new feature must answer one question:

> **Does this feature genuinely help users make better buying decisions?**

If the answer is **No**, the feature should not be built.

---

# Principle 1 — Trust Before Revenue

Trust is the foundation of Vywee.

Affiliate commissions, advertisements and sponsored content must never influence product rankings.

User trust always comes first.

---

# Principle 2 — Explain Every Recommendation

Never recommend something without explaining why.

Every recommendation should answer:

- Why this product?
- Why not another product?
- Who should buy it?
- Who should avoid it?

---

# Principle 3 — Show Trade-offs

There is rarely one perfect product.

Whenever appropriate, explain both strengths and weaknesses.

Example:

Instead of:

> Buy Product A.

Prefer:

> Product A is better if battery life matters.

> Product B is better if performance is your priority.

---

# Principle 4 — AI Assists, Humans Decide

AI helps users understand options.

AI should never pressure users into buying.

The final decision always belongs to the customer.

---

# Principle 5 — Transparency

Whenever information is estimated or generated by AI, it should be clearly indicated.

Users should understand the difference between:

- Verified data
- AI analysis
- Editorial opinion

---

# Principle 6 — Mobile First

Every feature must work beautifully on mobile devices.

Desktop enhancements come after the mobile experience.

---

# Principle 7 — Simplicity

Every screen should answer three questions within a few seconds.

1. What is this?
2. Why should I care?
3. What should I do next?

---

# Principle 8 — Performance

Fast products build trust.

Target goals:

- Homepage < 2 seconds
- Search feels instant
- Product pages load quickly
- Smooth scrolling
- Responsive interactions

---

# Principle 9 — Accessibility

Vywee should be usable by everyone.

Requirements include:

- Keyboard navigation
- Good color contrast
- Readable typography
- Screen reader support
- Touch-friendly controls

---

# Principle 10 — Honest Monetization

Sponsored content must always be clearly labelled.

Advertising must never be disguised as recommendations.

Affiliate relationships should never reduce recommendation quality.

---

# Principle 11 — Privacy

Collect only the information necessary to improve the experience.

Never sell user data.

Respect user choices.

---

# Principle 12 — Learn Continuously

The product should continuously improve through:

- User feedback
- Search trends
- Buying behavior (aggregated)
- AI improvements
- Editorial review

---

# What Vywee Should Feel Like

If Vywee were a person, it would be:

- Honest
- Calm
- Knowledgeable
- Helpful
- Transparent
- Trustworthy

Never:

- Pushy
- Clickbait
- Confusing
- Manipulative

---

# Success Metric

Success is not measured by clicks.

Success is measured by confidence.

A successful session ends with a user feeling:

> "Now I know what I should buy."

---

# Long-Term Vision

Vywee aims to become the first destination people visit before making any important purchase, whether online or in-store.

---

# Document Maintenance

This document should only change when a core philosophy of Vywee changes.

Feature requests must adapt to these principles—not the other way around.
