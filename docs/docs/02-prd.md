# Product Requirements Document

## Objective

Define every core feature required for Vywee MVP.

The goal is to build a platform that helps users confidently decide what to buy.

---

# MVP Scope

Included

- Homepage
- Search
- Product Page
- Compare
- Buying Guides
- My Research
- My Picks
- Ask Someone
- In-Store Assistant
- Vywee Studio

Excluded

- Rewards
- Cashback
- Browser Extension
- Public Profiles
- Community Feed
- Premium Subscription

---

# Homepage

## Goal

Help users begin their buying journey in under 10 seconds.

## Components

- Header
- Search Bar
- Categories
- Trending Products
- Vywee Choice
- Buying Guides
- Footer

### Primary CTA

Search

---

# Search

Supports:

- Natural language
- Category search
- Brand search
- Budget search

Example:

- Best phone under ₹30,000
- Laptop for coding
- Best AC for bedroom

Results include:

- AI Summary
- Products
- Buying Guides
- Compare

---

# Product Page

Must include:

- Product Images
- Specifications
- AI Summary
- Why Buy
- Why Skip
- Best For
- Alternatives
- Top 3 Offers
- View All Offers
- Price History
- Buying Guides
- Share
- Save
- Ask Someone

---

# Compare

Supports:

- 2 Products
- 3 Products
- 4 Products

Sections:

- Winner
- Trade-offs
- Feature Comparison
- Price Comparison
- AI Verdict
- Best For

---

# Buying Guides

Each guide includes:

- Introduction
- Who Should Buy
- Top Recommendations
- Comparison Table
- FAQs
- Related Products

---

# My Research

Stores:

- Saved Products
- Saved Comparisons
- Notes
- History

---

# My Picks

Allows users to create collections.

Examples:

- Office Setup
- Home Office
- Wedding Shopping
- Gaming Setup

Collections are shareable.

---

# Ask Someone

Workflow:

User selects product

↓

Creates share link

↓

Shares via WhatsApp, Email or Copy Link

↓

Recipient opens decision page

↓

Votes or comments

---

# In-Store Assistant

Purpose:

Help users while shopping offline.

Features:

- Search Product
- Scan Barcode (Future)
- Compare Online Prices
- Check Offers
- Ask Someone
- View Saved Research

---

# Vywee Studio

Internal dashboard for editors.

Modules:

- Dashboard
- Products
- Categories
- Brands
- Retailers
- Buying Guides
- AI Center
- Analytics

---

# Success Criteria

Users should answer:

"What should I buy?"

within five minutes.

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

Performance

- Homepage <2 seconds
- Mobile First
- Responsive Design
- SEO Optimized
- Accessible
- Secure
- Scalable
