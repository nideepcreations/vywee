# Product Vision

## Purpose

This document defines why Vywee exists, who it serves, and what success looks like.

It should help every designer, developer and future team member understand the long-term direction of the product.

---

# Vision Statement

**Become the world's most trusted buying decision platform.**

People should visit Vywee before making any important purchase.

---

# Mission

Help users make confident buying decisions by bringing together:

- Trusted research
- AI insights
- Product comparisons
- Price tracking
- Buying guides
- Community opinions

into one simple experience.

---

# Problem Statement

Today's buying journey is fragmented.

A typical customer visits multiple websites before purchasing a single product.

Example:

- Google
- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Brand Website
- YouTube
- Reddit
- Review Blogs

This process is slow, confusing and often biased.

Vywee solves this by becoming one trusted destination.

---

# Our Solution

Vywee combines everything needed before purchasing:

- AI-powered search
- Honest comparisons
- Live prices
- Buying guides
- Best offers
- Community opinions
- In-Store Assistant

Users spend less time researching and make better decisions.

Product success is measured by:

- User trust
- Repeat visitors

- Affiliate conversion
- Returning users

---

# North Star Metric

**Users who make a confident buying decision using Vywee.**

---

# Competitive Position

Vywee is not competing with Amazon.

Vywee helps users decide **before** purchasing.

Shopping platforms focus on transactions.

Vywee focuses on decisions.

---

# Future Vision

One day, users should naturally think:

> "Before I spend money, let me check Vywee."

That moment is the ultimate success of the platform.
